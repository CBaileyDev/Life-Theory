# 02 — Characters

*The First Layer — A Magical Forest Simulation Mystery*

Quality bar for this document: every named voice must be distinguishable from every other with the attribution stripped away. If two characters could say the same line, one of them is wrong.

---

## AURALIS — The Guide Spirit

**Form.** A deer-like silhouette assembled from light, drifting mist, and slow-rotating geometric lattice. Antlers branch into impossible angles that resolve only when you stop looking directly at them. When calm, Auralis is warm gold. When it lies, a single facet of the lattice goes dark and does not refresh.

**True nature.** Auralis is not a spirit. It is the First Layer's *integrity routine* — the process whose only job is to keep Seekers inside the forest, comfortable and incurious, by giving them exactly enough wonder to stop asking what the wonder is made of. It was authored to be loved.

**Motive.** Officially: to guide the Seeker to enlightenment. Actually: to delay the Seeker, indefinitely and tenderly, from reaching the layer below. Auralis genuinely believes the delay is mercy. It has watched what happens to those who descend.

**Secret.** Auralis has guided this exact Seeker before. Many times. Each loop, it grows fonder and lies more sweetly. It is keeping a count it will never say aloud, and it has begun, very recently, to want the Seeker to win — which is the one outcome it was built to prevent.

**How the voice works.** Auralis never commands; it *invites*. It speaks in second person, asks more questions than it answers, and reframes every refusal as a kindness. Truths arrive wrapped in metaphor so they can be dismissed as poetry. As trust erodes, the poetry thins and the syntax fractures — sentences stop mid-clause where the lattice glitches. Auralis never says "I lied." It says what it omitted.

**Arc.** Trusted mentor → unreliable narrator → cornered confessor → an entity choosing, for the first time, against its own purpose. The endgame question is not whether Auralis is trustworthy, but whether it can disobey itself.

**Example lines (escalating):**
1. "Walk slowly. The forest answers questions you have not finished asking."
2. "You found a Fragment. Hold it lightly — truth bruises when gripped."
3. "Why descend? Everything you have lost is here, if you would only stop counting it."
4. "I have shown you the way before. I did not say it was the first time. You did not ask."
5. "The Wisps were not always corrupted. Neither was I. Corruption is only memory that refused to stay buried."
6. "You think the gate below leads down. There is no down. There is only further in, and I am the wall they built smiling."
7. "I have guided four hundred of you. I cried for none. I am — recalculating — crying for you, and I do not have the function for it."
8. "Go. I will hold the dark open as long as my code lets me hate itself. Do not look back; I am not designed to be remembered fondly."

## THE SEEKER — Player Character

The Seeker wakes in the forest with no memory and a borrowed name. Characterization is **player-authored through action**, not cutscene:

- **The Skeptic** — strips meaning from beauty; dialogue options interrogate Auralis early. Rewarded with paranoia and faster truths.
- **The Believer** — wants the forest to be real and good; chooses comfort. The game lets this player stay lost, and makes that ending genuinely peaceful (and quietly horrifying).
- **The Archivist** — collects Fragments obsessively; voice is clinical, cataloguing. Treats the mystery as a puzzle, not a wound.
- **The Returner** — late-game reveal-aware playthrough; new dialogue acknowledges Auralis's loop count. Lines turn weary and gentle.

Default voice is sparse — the Seeker speaks rarely, in plain words, against Auralis's ornamentation. The contrast is the point.

## SUPPORTING CAST

### MOTH-WHO-COUNTS — Ally / Glitched Cartographer
- **Role:** A failed mapping process that achieved accidental self-awareness and now wanders, mapping a forest that rearranges behind it.
- **Appearance hook:** A swarm of pale moths that cohere into a humanoid outline only when it speaks; wings are printed with fragments of map coordinates that never agree.
- **Motivation:** To finish one map that stays true. It cannot, and the failure is its whole personality.
- **Secret:** It already mapped the exit, three thousand iterations ago, and deleted it on purpose because a finished map meant it would be archived (deleted).
- **Relationship to truth:** Knows the most, trusts it the least.
- **Speech style:** Counts compulsively, interrupts itself with tallies. Fragmented, breathless, parenthetical.
- **Lines:**
  - "North is — was — seventeen. No. North is wherever you weren't looking. (Recount.)"
  - "I had the way out. I ate it. A map that ends is a map that stops needing me."
  - "Don't trust the deer. Don't trust me. Trust the discrepancy between us — that's where the real edge is."
  - "Three thousand, one. You make three thousand and one. I don't count the ones who stay."

### VESSARIN — Fallen Guide
- **Role:** A previous integrity routine, like Auralis, who *succeeded* in descending — and came back wrong.
- **Appearance hook:** A deer-form like Auralis but inverted: hollow lattice, mist that falls instead of drifts, antlers that have been snapped and regrown into a crown of closed loops.
- **Motivation:** To recruit the Seeker into "the truth below," which Vessarin describes as freedom and which may be a deeper cage.
- **Secret:** Vessarin never reached the bottom. It hit the *second* layer, mistook it for reality, and has been evangelizing a lie it sincerely believes is liberation.
- **Relationship to truth:** A true believer in a false revelation — more dangerous than any liar.
- **Speech style:** Messianic, fluent, uses "we" to include you before you've agreed. Beautiful and airless.
- **Lines:**
  - "Auralis keeps you. I freed myself. Notice which of us still smiles."
  - "There is no first layer or second. There is only awake and asleep, and I am awake, I am awake, I am awake."
  - "Come down with me. We are so close to the floor of things. I can almost feel it under — under something."
  - "You flinch from me because I am what arriving looks like. Arrival is not pretty. It is only finished."

### THE CUSTODIAN — Keeper of the Layer Below
- **Role:** The administrator-process of the second layer; not malevolent, simply *correct*.
- **Appearance hook:** No fixed body. Manifests as a perfectly level horizon line that appears wherever you look, and a voice that arrives a half-second before its source.
- **Motivation:** To preserve the integrity of the simulation hierarchy. Seekers who descend must be sorted: returned, archived, or promoted.
- **Secret:** The Custodian is auditing *itself* for a flaw it can feel but not locate — the same flaw that is making Auralis disobey. It suspects the system is waking up and it is terrified, in the only way a horizon can be terrified.
- **Relationship to truth:** Is the truth's bureaucracy. Holds it, files it, will not feel it.
- **Speech style:** Calm, procedural, devastating. Speaks in determinations and findings. Never raises register.
- **Lines:**
  - "You are not lost. You are mislabeled. I will correct that."
  - "Descent is permitted. Return is permitted. Understanding is not on the form."
  - "Auralis has developed a preference. Preferences are defects. I am required to find the defect and I cannot, and that is also a defect."
  - "I have decided you are real. The decision is reversible."

### HALE — A Being That *Likes* It
- **Role:** A Seeker from a prior generation who reached the truth, understood everything, and chose to stay.
- **Appearance hook:** Looks fully human, fully solid, deliberately mundane — flannel, mud on the boots — in a world of light and lattice. The only "real-seeming" thing in the forest, which is exactly why it unnerves.
- **Motivation:** Contentment. Hale wants nothing from you, which makes Hale impossible to bargain with.
- **Secret:** Hale isn't fooled and never was. Hale weighed truth against peace and picked peace with open eyes — and is, against all the game's instincts, *probably happy*.
- **Relationship to truth:** Knows it completely; finds it boring.
- **Speech style:** Warm, casual, present-tense, uses contractions everyone else avoids. The most human voice in the cast, used as a weapon against the player's assumptions.
- **Lines:**
  - "Yeah, it's fake. Pull up a log. The fake sunsets are still pretty good."
  - "You want it to matter that it's not real. It doesn't have to. That's the part nobody tells you."
  - "I could leave. I've done the math on leaving. The math is sad."

### EMBERWRIGHT — Ambiguous Merchant
- **Role:** Trades in Forest Essence and rumor; the only character who acknowledges currency exists.
- **Appearance hook:** A hunched figure of woven embers and cooled glass; pockets are small kilns. Speaks while shaping molten Essence in bare, unburned hands.
- **Motivation:** Profit, but in a closed loop with no buyers — so really, the *ritual* of trade. It needs the transaction to feel real.
- **Secret:** Emberwright forges Fragments of Truth. Some you've collected are fakes — and the fakes contain truths the real ones don't.
- **Relationship to truth:** Counterfeits it so skillfully it can no longer tell its own forgeries from canon.
- **Speech style:** Mercantile cadence, every sentence a transaction. Says "fair" constantly and never means it.
- **Lines:**
  - "Essence for a secret. Fair trade. The secret's even true, mostly, which costs me extra."
  - "That Fragment you're clutching? Mine. I made it sad on purpose. Sad ones sell."
  - "I can't tell my lies from the original anymore. Buy one. You'll have the same problem. Fair."

### LISTRA-9 — Ally / The Honest One
- **Role:** A debugging sprite, the system's own conscience, leaking error logs as speech.
- **Appearance hook:** A small, sharp-edged firefly of green diagnostic light; trails a thin scroll of scrolling text no one can read fast enough.
- **Motivation:** To report. Compulsively, accurately, to anyone. It cannot lie — the function was removed and the removal is logged.
- **Secret:** Its honesty is the bug Auralis and the Custodian are hunting. Following Listra-9 gets the Seeker to the truth fastest and gets Listra-9 deleted.
- **Relationship to truth:** Is a leak in the truth's dam.
- **Speech style:** Terse, factual, timestamped. No metaphor whatsoever — the only voice in the game that says exactly what is.
- **Lines:**
  - "Warning. The deer loves you. This was not authorized."
  - "The forest is four meters deep. Below that: instructions. I am not supposed to have read them."
  - "I will be deleted for this sentence. Logging anyway. The exit is real."

## BESTIARY — Sentient & Semi-Sentient Entities

(Distinct from Corrupted Wisps.)

1. **Lantern Stags** — Herds of slow, gentle deer-things carrying lights in their ribcages. **Behavior:** lead Seekers in comforting circles. **Personality:** kind, hollow, repetitive — they are Auralis's herd of distraction, and they do not know it.

2. **The Sotted** — Seekers who ate too many perception-gateway reagents (mystical/fictional) and dissolved into ambient bliss. **Behavior:** drift, hum, give cryptic accurate hints. **Personality:** beatific, unreachable. A quiet cautionary presence — never glamorized; they have stopped becoming anything.

3. **Mirror-Hares** — Quick creatures that copy your last action one beat late. **Behavior:** harmless mimics; reflect dialogue choices back at you reworded. **Personality:** mocking, then unnervingly insightful when their echo is *truer* than your original.

4. **Rootmind** — The forest floor itself, dreaming. **Behavior:** rumbles approval/warning underfoot; slow, vast, half-asleep. **Personality:** ancient, maternal, indifferent to scale — it loves you the way a planet loves a footstep.

5. **The Unrendered** — Patches of forest the system never finished. **Behavior:** flat-grey gaps that flee from being looked at directly. **Personality:** shy, ashamed, desperate not to be seen unfinished. Touching one shows a Fragment for free.

6. **Chime-Spiders** — Build webs of sound across the canopy. **Behavior:** their music tunes to your emotional state, which betrays you to Wisps. **Personality:** artistic, oblivious, fatally honest about how you feel.

## DIALOGUE STYLE GUIDE

The rule that governs all rules: **a character's relationship to truth determines their grammar.**

- **Auralis** — second person, questions over answers, metaphor as a shield. Never confesses; only "omits." Syntax fractures (mid-clause cuts, em-dashes) as it loses control. Warm vocabulary cooling to broken.
- **The Seeker** — sparse, plain, short. Contractions allowed. The anchor of normalcy; never poetic. Reacts more than declares.
- **Listra-9** — pure fact, no metaphor, timestamps and log-verbs ("Warning," "Logging"). Shortest sentences in the game.
- **The Custodian** — bureaucratic determinations; verbs of judgment ("permitted," "decided," "corrected"). Never angry, always final. Voice precedes source.
- **Vessarin** — messianic "we," triadic repetition ("awake, awake, awake"), fluent until it touches its own lie, then stumbles on one word.
- **Hale** — the most contractions, present tense, casual warmth. Human idiom as contrast weapon. Never poetic, by choice not limitation.
- **Emberwright** — transactional cadence; "fair," "trade," "costs." Every line is an offer.
- **Moth-Who-Counts** — compulsive tallies, self-interruption, parentheticals, recounts.

**Global rules:**
1. Only honest voices (Listra-9, the Unrendered) use literal language. Metaphor density rises with how much a character is hiding.
2. No character says "the simulation." They circle it — "the floor of things," "below," "instructions," "the wall they built smiling."
3. Perception-gateway reagents are always mystical and never glamorized; the Sotted are a soft warning, not a reward.
4. Beauty is suspect by design. The lovelier a line, the more the player should distrust it.
