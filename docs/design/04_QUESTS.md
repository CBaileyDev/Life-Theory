# The First Layer — Quest Framework

> Tone: poetic, unsettling, layered-reality mystery. The forest is real until it isn't. Every "truth" you collect is also a wound in the world's pretending. Auralis guides — but guides toward *what*?

---

## 0. Pillars

- **The Sight** — Fragments of Truth refine your perception. Each tier of Sight reveals what the simulation paints over: hidden geometry, ghost-text, the seams of the layer. Sight is mystical/fictional ("the mushroom-light opens the eye behind the eye"). Higher Sight = more truth, less comfort.
- **Descent** — The game is structured as layers, not levels. You go *down* into the simulation, never out. Each Act = one layer, rendered with lower fidelity and stranger rules.
- **Auralis** — your guide-spirit, fragmented across layers. Her motives drift as you descend. Trust is a resource.

---

## 1. Main Quest Spine

The spine spans **5 layers / Acts**, 8 main quests. The vertical slice is **MQ1**.

### ACT I — THE GREENWOOD (Layer 1: the lie that feels like home)

**MQ1 — The First Layer** *(existing slice)*
- **Premise:** Wake beneath the canopy with no memory. A glowing mushroom pulses nearby.
- **Steps:** Find glowing mushroom → consume to gain first Sight → meet Auralis → collect 3 Fragments of Truth → return to the Shrine → choose first upgrade (Rootblade reach / Sight radius / Essence yield).
- **Beat/Twist:** At the Shrine, Sight flickers — for one frame the trees render as wireframe. Auralis pretends not to notice.
- **Reward:** Ancient Rootblade unlocked; first upgrade; Sight Tier 1.
- **Advances mystery:** Establishes that perception is a mechanic and the forest is constructed.

**MQ2 — The Cartographer of Smoke**
- **Premise:** A second wanderer, **Veil**, claims to have mapped the Greenwood — but his map shows paths that don't exist *yet*.
- **Steps:** Follow Veil's map → reach a clearing that only appears under Sight → defend Veil from a Corrupted Wisp swarm → recover his "true map" (a Fragment-encoded glyph sheet).
- **Beat/Twist:** Veil's map is *predictive* — the forest builds itself to match it. He's not mapping the world; the world is obeying the map. Veil begs you to stop drawing.
- **Reward:** Glyph-reading ability (enables set-piece puzzles); Essence cache.
- **Advances:** Proves the layer is authored in real time, and the player may be the author.

### ACT II — THE HOLLOW ROOTS (Layer 2: where the seams show)

**MQ3 — The Shrine That Remembers**
- **Premise:** The Shrine speaks in your own voice, recalling choices you don't remember making.
- **Steps:** Descend through a root-tunnel (first deliberate "going down") → light 4 memory-braziers in the correct emotional order (puzzle) → confront a recording of a "previous you."
- **Beat/Twist:** The previous-you completed this game already and chose to stay. They warn you: *"Auralis lies kindly."*
- **Reward:** Sight Tier 2 (reveals ghost-text on NPCs — their scripted intentions); the **Echo flag** is set (prior-loop awareness).
- **Advances:** Introduces the loop; Auralis's trustworthiness becomes a tracked variable.

**MQ4 — The Wisp That Wept**
- **Premise:** A single Corrupted Wisp refuses to attack and instead leads you somewhere.
- **Steps:** Follow without striking (combat-restraint objective) → reach a drowned grove → learn Wisps are *deleted NPCs*, corrupted memory the simulation couldn't garbage-collect.
- **Beat/Twist:** You can **purify** the Wisp (restore it, gain an ally) or **harvest** it (max Essence, but it screams a name — possibly yours). This sets the **Mercy/Harvest axis**.
- **Reward:** Ally companion *or* large Essence + Rootblade corruption-infusion (stronger, hungrier weapon).
- **Advances:** Reframes the entire enemy faction as victims; first hard moral fork.

### ACT III — THE LATTICE (Layer 3: unreliable geometry)

**MQ5 — The Library of Unwritten Names**
- **Premise:** Auralis sends you to retrieve her "true name" from a library where the architecture loops.
- **Steps:** Navigate a non-Euclidean library (looping-space set-piece) → assemble her name from scattered Fragments → read it aloud.
- **Beat/Twist:** Her name is a **system process ID**. Auralis is not a spirit — she's the layer's caretaker daemon. Speaking her name gives you partial admin Sight... and lets her see *you* clearly for the first time.
- **Reward:** Sight Tier 3 (see triggers/colliders as glowing seams); **Auralis-Suspicion** branch opens.
- **Advances:** The guide is unmasked as machinery; player gains agency over the simulation.

**MQ6 — The Gardener's Confession**
- **Premise:** Find who *planted* the first layer. Trail leads to the **Gardener**, a hermit at the lattice's edge.
- **Steps:** Trade Fragments for confession fragments (no fetch-padding — each Fragment shown triggers a distinct memory cutscene) → choose which truths to believe.
- **Beat/Twist:** The Gardener is a *player from a deeper layer* who built the Greenwood as a refuge to forget a trauma — and you are their attempt to face it.
- **Reward:** The **Descent Key** (access to Layer 4); the **Identity flag** (who you are to the Gardener).
- **Advances:** Recontextualizes the protagonist as constructed-for-a-purpose.

### ACT IV — THE SUBSTRATE (Layer 4: low-fidelity raw simulation)

**MQ7 — The Quiet Below**
- **Premise:** Layer 4 is barely rendered — gray plates, placeholder trees, the Rootblade leaving no particle effects. Auralis's voice degrades.
- **Steps:** Cross the unrendered expanse using only Sight-seams as footing → reach the **Core Shrine** → decide whether to repair the failing layer or let it collapse.
- **Beat/Twist:** Collapsing Layer 4 *frees* the Corrupted Wisps but kills every NPC you befriended above. Repairing it traps everyone, kindly, forever.
- **Reward:** Final Sight Tier 4 (true sight — render the world as code); positions for endings.
- **Advances:** Forces the Mercy/Harvest and Trust axes to pay off.

### ACT V — THE FIRST LAYER (Layer 5 / 0: the floor that is also the ceiling)

**MQ8 — The First Layer (Reprise)**
- **Premise:** The deepest layer is identical to the opening clearing — the same mushroom, the same Auralis. The descent was a circle.
- **Steps:** Re-enact the opening with full Sight → the mushroom is now clearly a *save-point beacon* → make the Final Choice at the original Shrine.
- **Beat/Twist:** "Down" was always "back to the start." You can break the loop, reset it kindly, or become the next Gardener/Auralis for the next wanderer.
- **Reward:** Ending. The first upgrade screen returns — but now you understand what it was choosing.
- **Advances:** Resolves the mystery: the simulation has no bottom; only the choice of what to do with that.

## 2. Side Quests

1. **The Borrowed Face** — A villager wears *your* face. Hook: they insist they're the original. Dilemma: expose them (they dissolve) or let them keep the identity (you lose a Sight tier briefly, "loaning" your selfhood). Reward: Essence or a unique Sight-lens.
2. **Mushrooms That Remember Rain** — Plant Sight-mushrooms in dry zones. Hook: they only grow where someone once cried. Dilemma: harvest for Sight or leave them as memorials (unlocks lore murals). Reward: Sight charges / lore.
3. **The Honest Wisp** — A Wisp answers questions truthfully but each answer corrupts it further. Dilemma: how many truths is one creature's mind worth? Reward: 3 deep-lore answers, scaled to chosen cost.
4. **The Cartographer's Apprentice** — Veil's student draws a door. Hook: it opens. Dilemma: walk through (skip ahead a layer, miss content) or seal it (Veil rewards your restraint). Reward: shortcut or Essence + map.
5. **Echoes in the Essence** — Spend Forest Essence at a "wishing pool" and hear a past-loop player's voice. Dilemma: spend savings for cryptic endgame hints, or hoard. Reward: ending foreshadowing.
6. **The Tree That Counts** — A tree tallies every Wisp you've killed. Hook: it asks if the number feels right. Dilemma: lie (it grows fruit) or confess (it withers, gives a true Fragment). Reward: Fragment vs. consumable.
7. **Two Auralis** — A second Auralis appears, claiming the first is the corrupted one. Dilemma: choose which to trust; affects Auralis-Trust flag. Reward: gear from whichever you side with.
8. **The Unfinished NPC** — A villager with placeholder dialogue ("[GREETING_LINE]"). Hook: under Sight they beg you to write their personality. Dilemma: author them (they become a vendor) or leave them blank (they wander free, occasionally helping in combat). Reward: vendor or roaming ally.
9. **The Recursion Garden** — A side-shrine where collecting a Fragment spawns a copy of the room. Dilemma: how deep do you nest before the frame-rate-prayer (a stability meter) breaks? Reward: scaling Essence, risk of a "soft reset" mini-penalty.
10. **The Last True Thing** — Hidden NPC claims one object in the forest is *not* simulated. Hook: a quest to find the real thing. Dilemma: when found (a plain stone), keep it (passive Sight clarity) or shatter it to prove nothing is real (permanent eerie audio layer). Reward: buff or atmosphere unlock.

## 3. Puzzle / Exploration Set-Pieces

- **Glyph-Code Braziers (MQ3):** Four braziers encode an emotional sequence (grief → doubt → wonder → resolve) shown as Veil-glyphs only visible under Sight. Wrong order spawns a Wisp wave; correct order plays a memory. Teaches reading the world's hidden language.
- **The Looping Library (MQ5):** A space that repeats unless you carry a Fragment, which "anchors" reality. Doorways lead back to themselves until the player notices a single drifting detail (a falling leaf that never lands) marking the true exit. Sight reveals the seam connecting room A's far door to room A's near door.
- **The Unrendered Crossing (MQ7):** Only Sight-seam edges are solid footing; the rest is void that drops you (non-lethal, resets to last seam). A real-time exploration puzzle where *perception is traversal*. Optional: spend Essence to "render" a bridge (easier, but consumes the resource that fuels endings).

## 4. Quest-State / Flag Model (implementation)

**Quest object**
```
Quest { id, act, state: [Locked|Available|Active|Branching|Complete|Failed],
        objectives[], prerequisites[], rewards[], flagsSet[], flagsRequired[] }
```

**Objective types**
- `Reach(zoneId)` — trigger volume entry.
- `Collect(itemId, count)` — inventory threshold (Fragments, Essence).
- `Interact(entityId)` — NPC/shrine/object use.
- `Defeat(enemyTag, count)` / `Survive(seconds)` — combat.
- `Restraint(actionTag, withhold=true)` — succeed by *not* doing X (MQ4, Honest Wisp).
- `Sequence(stepIds[], ordered=true)` — glyph/brazier puzzles.
- `Choice(optionId)` — sets a branching flag; never auto-resolves.

**Triggers:** `OnEnterVolume`, `OnFlagSet`, `OnItemThreshold`, `OnDialogueNode`, `OnSightTier(n)`, `OnQuestComplete(id)`. Triggers evaluate against the global flag store each tick or on event.

**Branching:** A quest in `Branching` state holds open `Choice` objectives. Resolving one writes to flags and selects a `rewards`/`flagsSet` set. Downstream quests gate on `flagsRequired`.

**Key flags (booleans/enums):**
`Sight_Tier (0–4)`, `Auralis_Trust (-3…+3)`, `Mercy_Harvest (-N…+N, Wisp tally)`, `Echo_Aware (bool)`, `Identity (Stranger|Gardener_Kin|Self)`, `Layer4_Choice (Repair|Collapse)`, `True_Things_Kept (int)`.

## 5. Choices → Endings

Endings are computed at MQ8 from three weighted axes plus key flags.

- **Axis A — Mercy/Harvest** (how you treated Wisps/NPCs).
- **Axis B — Auralis Trust** (did you obey, doubt, or unmask her).
- **Axis C — Layer4 + Identity** (repair vs. collapse; who you accept you are).

**Endings:**
1. **The Kind Loop** — High Mercy + Repair. You reset the layer gently and stay as the new Auralis. Comforting, complicit.
2. **The Clean Break** — High Harvest + Collapse + Distrust. You shatter the simulation; ambiguous black screen — freedom or deletion.
3. **The Gardener's Heir** — Identity = Gardener_Kin + balanced axes. You inherit the garden, vow to build a *truer* refuge for the next wanderer.
4. **Eyes Open** — Sight Tier 4 + many True_Things_Kept + high Trust earned-through-doubt. You stay awake inside the lie, neither fixing nor breaking it — the only one who *sees* and chooses to keep watching.
5. **Secret — The Author** — Spent every Essence wish (SQ5) + refused all upgrades. The opening upgrade screen reappears and *you* become the menu the next player chooses from.

Each ending replays the opening clearing differently, so the circle closes on the note the player earned.
